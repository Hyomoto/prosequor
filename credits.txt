Adding a new icon
-----------------
  1. Download from the icon's page, not a random mirror.
  2. Keep the original slug in icons/{slug}.svg (scratch folder).
  3. Copy/rename into assets/prosequor/textures/icons/ as needed.
  4. Add a row in the matching section: shipped path, original title,
     author, page URL (or "original" if you drew it).
  5. Note extras (recolor, crop, composite). Do not mix sources
     across sections.
